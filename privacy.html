Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

TradeLog NG. Last updated: 4 November 2026.

In short: TradeLog NG helps you record sales, debts and expenses. Your records belong to you. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we only use your information to run the app for you.

1. Who we are

TradeLog NG ("we", "us") is a record-keeping app for small traders in Nigeria. You can contact us at tradelogng@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • Your account: your email, password (stored in protected form, we cannot read it), full name, shop name, phone number and optional profile picture.
  • Your business records: sales, expenses, debts and payments, with their dates and times.
  • Your customers' details: names and phone numbers you type in when you record a debt.
  • On your phone only: a saved copy of your records so the app works without network, and your PIN (if you set one). The PIN is never sent to us.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, your bank details or your BVN. If we add paid plans later, payments will be handled by a licensed payment company such as Paystack, and we will update this page.

3. How we use it

  • To show you your records, totals, reports and reminders.
  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.

4. Where your data is kept

Your records are stored with our database provider, Supabase, on servers in Europe (Ireland). The app itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Each account can only see its own records. A copy is also kept on your phone so the app works offline.

5. Who can see your data

  • Only you, when you are signed in.
  • Our service providers (such as Supabase and GitHub), only as needed to run the app.
  • Authorities, only if the law in Nigeria requires us to share it.

We never sell your data or your customers' data.

6. WhatsApp reminders, receipts and summaries

When you tap "Send on WhatsApp", the app opens WhatsApp on your phone with the message ready. You decide whether to send it, and it is sent from your own WhatsApp. We do not read your WhatsApp chats. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies once the message is sent.

7. Your customers' information

You decide which customer details to record, so you are responsible for them. Please only record what you need, use it for your business records and reminders, and do not misuse it. If a customer asks you to correct or remove their details, you can edit or delete the debt in the app.

8. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, and you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC). In the app you can:

  • Edit your profile, debts, sales and expenses.
  • Download your records from the Reports tab (spreadsheet or PDF).
  • Remove records one by one, or use "Clear all my records" if your version of the app has it.

To delete your whole account, email us from your registered email address. We will normally do it within 30 days.

9. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections and each account is separated from the others. The PIN lock only stops other people from opening the app on your phone. Please keep your phone and password safe, and sign out if you share your phone. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care.

10. Children

TradeLog NG is for business use by adults aged 18 and above.

11. Terms of use

  • Use it honestly. Do not use TradeLog NG for anything illegal, to harass people, or to try to break into other accounts.
  • It is a record-keeping tool. It is not accounting, tax or legal advice. Please check important figures yourself.
  • Back up your records. Download your reports from time to time, because we cannot promise the app will never fail or lose data.
  • Free for now. The app is free during this early period. If we introduce paid plans, we will tell you before any charge, and you will choose whether to pay.
  • Accounts. We may suspend an account that breaks these terms. You can stop using the app at any time.
  • Our responsibility. We provide the app "as it is". To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for business losses caused by errors, downtime or lost data.
  • Law. These terms are governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

12. Changes

If we change this page in an important way, we will tell you in the app or by email. The date at the top shows the latest version.

13. Contact

Questions, or want your data deleted? Email tradelogng@gmail.com.
